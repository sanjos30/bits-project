1. **Load Production Data** (1M+ transactions)

3. **Select User** (Wayne Morgan - 59, retiree)
4. **Run AI Queries** (5 different questions)
5. **Validate Responses** (Compare with real data)
6. **Show Processing Time** (~4-5 seconds)

2. "What is my total income?"

5. "What are my top spending categories?"
