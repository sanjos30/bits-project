## 🤔 Anticipated Questions & Answers

### Technical Questions

**Q: Why DistilGPT2 instead of larger models?**
A: CPU compatibility for local deployment, faster training, adequate performance for financial queries, cost-effective for production.

**Q: How do you ensure data privacy?**
A: Local deployment means no data leaves the user's device, encrypted storage, no cloud transmission, complete user control.

**Q: What's the accuracy compared to human analysis?**
A: 94% accuracy on test queries, with human-like reasoning capabilities, and 100% reliability through hybrid system.

**Q: How does the hybrid system work?**
A: Primary responses from trained model, automatic fallback to rule-based system if confidence is low, ensuring 100% uptime.

### Business Questions

**Q: How do you plan to acquire users?**
A: B2B partnerships with banks, freemium model for B2C, content marketing, referral programs.

**Q: What's your competitive advantage?**
A: Privacy-first design, hybrid reliability, personalized insights, local deployment architecture.

**Q: How do you handle regulatory compliance?**
A: Local deployment reduces compliance burden, data encryption, audit trails, RBI guidelines compliance.

**Q: What's your go-to-market strategy?**
A: Start with B2B partnerships for validation, then expand to B2C, focus on privacy-conscious users.

## 💡 Presentation Tips

### Before the Presentation
- ✅ **Practice timing** - aim for 15-18 minutes
- ✅ **Review technical details** - be ready for deep questions
- ✅ **Prepare backup slides** - for detailed explanations
- ✅ **Test equipment** - ensure everything works

### During the Presentation
- ✅ **Speak clearly** - moderate pace, clear pronunciation
- ✅ **Make eye contact** - engage with committee members
- ✅ **Use gestures** - emphasize key points
- ✅ **Pause briefly** - between sections for clarity
- ✅ **Show confidence** - you've done excellent work!

### Handling Questions
- ✅ **Listen carefully** - understand the question fully
- ✅ **Answer directly** - address the specific concern
- ✅ **Use examples** - illustrate with your results
- ✅ **Be honest** - if you don't know, say so
- ✅ **Stay calm** - questions show interest in your work
